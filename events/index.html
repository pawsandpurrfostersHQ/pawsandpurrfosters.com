A little more information

Adoption Events & Locations

Come meet our foster kittens and other adoptable cats in person at one of No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue’s adoption events. Our fosters usually attend an event once a week on the weekend, where you can get to know the kittens, ask questions, learn about the adoption process, and see which kitten you connect with. All adoption events and adoptions are hosted through No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. If you are interested in adopting, visit our How to Adopt page for more information.

A little reminder when meeting our kittens

Adoption events can be busy and overwhelming for kittens, especially during their first few visits. A kitten may be shy, quiet, or more reserved at an event than they are in the comfort of their foster home.

It's important to remember that their behavior in a busy event setting may not fully reflect their personality. Many kittens become more comfortable, playful, and affectionate once they're settled into a familiar, loving home.

We encourage you to look beyond first impressions, take your time getting to know them, and remember that their personalities continue to shine as they grow more comfortable.
