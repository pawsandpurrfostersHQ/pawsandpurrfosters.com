Adoption Events & Locations

Come meet our foster kittens and other adoptable cats in person at one of our adoption events. We usually attend an event on the weekend, where you can get to know the kittens, ask questions, and learn more about adoption. All adoption events and adoptions are hosted through No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. If you are interested in adopting, visit our How to Adopt page for more information.

Please remember that adoption events can be busy and overwhelming for kittens, especially when they only attend occasionally. A kitten may be shy, quiet, or more reserved at an event than they are in their familiar foster home. Their event-day behavior may not show their full personality.
