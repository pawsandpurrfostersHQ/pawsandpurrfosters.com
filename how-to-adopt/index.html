How to Adopt

Paws & Purr Fosters is dedicated to helping every kitten find a loving home that is the perfect fit for them and for you and your family. We are a foster home working in partnership with No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The kittens in our care are placed with us through the rescue, and all adoption applications, approvals, contracts, adoption fees, and final adoptions are handled by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. Our role is to provide a safe, loving foster home, get to know each kitten, and help you find the right match for your home.

1

Find the right kitten(s)

Find a kitten you're interested in and read their profile to get to know them and see whether they're a good fit for your home.

Most of our kittens are required to be adopted in pairs, so be sure to read about their bonded or required pair as well and make sure both kittens are a good fit for your family.

2

Meet the kitten

We always recommend a meet-and-greet before adoption whenever possible. This gives you an opportunity to meet the kitten, interact with them, ask questions, and make sure they are a good fit for your family.

A meet-and-greet may take place at an adoption event or at another arranged location, depending on the circumstances.

3

Submit an adoption application

There are two ways to get an adoption application through No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue:

  • Visit the rescue’s CONTACT US page and ask the rescue to send you the appropriate application and submission instructions.
  • Visit the rescue’s ADOPTION FORMS page to download an editable application to complete electronically or a printable copy. You can complete the editable form and email it to nopawsleftbehindkittyrescue@gmail.com, or print the form, fill it out by hand, then scan and email it to the same address. You may also bring a completed paper application to an in-person adoption event. The rescue will review your application and contact you with its decision. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or reserve a kitten.

No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue reviews all applications and will contact you with its decision. Submitting an application or asking for an application does not guarantee approval or reserve a kitten. Only a completed application submitted to the rescue can be reviewed.

4

Application review

Your application will be reviewed by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The rescue will contact you directly to let you know whether your application has been approved. Application decisions are made by the rescue, not by our foster team.

5

Adoption contract

If your application is approved, you will need to complete an adoption contract. The contract is most commonly completed in person at the adoption event or another location arranged by the rescue.

6

Pay the adoption fee

Adoption fees are collected by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue as part of finalizing the adoption. The rescue handles the entire payment process and will provide payment details and instructions. Please follow the rescue’s directions.

7

Bring your new family member home

Once the contract, adoption fee, and remaining adoption steps have been completed through the rescue, the rescue will coordinate the adoption and arrangements for your kitten to go home.

Your kitten(s) will come with a blue take-home folder containing their medical information and care guide, along with an adoption certificate and available store coupons.

A cat carrier is required for every kitten going home. Please bring your own carrier or arrange to purchase one. Neither the rescue nor our foster team provides carriers.

All adoption applications, application reviews and approvals, contracts, adoption fees, and final adoptions are handled by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. Our role is to provide a safe and loving foster home, get to know each kitten, and help you find the right match for your home.
