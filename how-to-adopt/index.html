How to Adopt

Paws & Purr Fosters is dedicated to helping every kitten find a loving home that is the perfect fit for them and for you and your family. We are a foster home working in partnership with No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The kittens in our care are placed with us through the rescue, and all adoption applications, approvals, contracts, adoption fees, and final adoptions are handled by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. Our role is to provide a safe, loving foster home, get to know each kitten, and help you find the right match for your home.

1

Find the right kitten(s)

Browse the kittens and read their profiles to learn about their personalities, needs, and whether they would be a good fit for your family.

Most of our kittens are required to be adopted in pairs. Be sure to read about each kitten’s bonded or required pair, too, and make sure both kittens are a good fit for your family.

2

Meet the kitten(s)

We always recommend a meet-and-greet before adoption whenever possible. It gives you a chance to meet the kitten(s), spend time with them, ask questions, and make sure they are a good fit for your family.

A meet-and-greet may take place at an adoption event or at another location arranged with the rescue and foster team.

3

Submit an adoption application

There are two ways to obtain an adoption application through No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue:

  • Visit the rescue’s CONTACT US page and complete the contact form. The rescue will reply by email with an application and instructions for submitting it.
  • Visit the rescue’s ADOPTION FORMS page to choose an editable application to complete electronically or a printable application to print and fill out by hand.

There are several ways to submit your completed application:

  • Email the completed electronic application to nopawsleftbehindkittyrescue@gmail.com.
  • If you use the printable form, fill it out by hand, then scan and email the completed application to the same address.
  • Bring your completed paper application with you to an in-person adoption event.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or reserve a kitten(s).

4

Application review

All applications are reviewed by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. Adoption decisions are made through a collaborative effort between the rescue and our foster team. The rescue will contact you directly with its decision.

5

Adoption contract

If your application is approved, you will need to complete an adoption contract. The contract is most commonly completed in person at the adoption event or another location arranged by the rescue.

6

Pay the adoption fee

Adoption fees are set and managed by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. For questions about the adoption fee, please email the rescue directly. The rescue handles the entire payment process and will provide payment details and instructions.

7

Bring your new family member home

Once your application is approved, the adoption contract and fee are completed, and all remaining steps are finalized, your kitten(s) are ready to go home!

  • Adopting at an event? If everything is finalized that day, you may take your kitten(s) home with you before you leave.
  • Adopting outside an event? The rescue and our foster team will coordinate a pickup time and any remaining arrangements with you.
  • Your take-home folder: Your kitten(s) will leave with a blue folder containing their medical information, care guide, adoption certificate, microchip number, and any available store coupons.
  • Bring a cat carrier: A carrier is required for every kitten going home. Please bring your own or arrange to purchase one before pickup. Carriers are not provided by the rescue or our foster team.

All adoption applications, application reviews and approvals, contracts, adoption fees, and final adoptions are handled by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. Our role is to provide a safe and loving foster home, get to know each kitten, and help you find the right match for your home.
