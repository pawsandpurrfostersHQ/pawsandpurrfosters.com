Why do we ask so many questions?

We ask these questions so we can look beyond a kitten’s photo and find a thoughtful fit for your home and the pets and people who already live there. Energy level, personality, affection, and how much time you can allow for introductions all matter. Whether you are open to adopting a bonded pair can matter, too. Our goal is to help you find a companion whose needs and personality fit your household, and to be honest when we do not have enough information to recommend a match confidently.

If you do not see the right fit among our kittens, we encourage you to explore the other cats and kittens available through No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue on Petfinder. The kittens featured here are the ones we personally foster. The rescue also has many other wonderful kittens and cats cared for by fellow foster homes.
