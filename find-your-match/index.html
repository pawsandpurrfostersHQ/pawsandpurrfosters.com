A few questions to help us find a thoughtful match.

Why do we ask so many questions?

We want to consider more than energy level or who looks cutest in a photo. Your existing pets, how slowly you can introduce a new kitten, the amount of affection you’re hoping for, and whether you’re open to a pair can all matter. We’ll be honest when we don’t have enough information to recommend a match confidently.
