Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our foster kittens or the adoption process? Here are a few helpful answers. If your question isn’t answered here, or you have a question about one of our kittens, you’re welcome to send us a message below.

Do bonded pairs need to be adopted together?

Yes. Here’s how pairing works:

  • Kittens labeled as a bonded pair must be adopted together, regardless of age.
  • Some kittens have a preferred pair listed on their profile, while others may be able to go home with a sibling or foster sibling.
  • Some single kittens may be adopted on their own, while others may need a home with another cat.
  • The pairing requirement for each kitten will be clearly listed on their profile. Most of our kittens need to go home in pairs.
  • No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue also requires kittens six months old and younger to be adopted in pairs.
Can I meet a kitten before applying?

Yes! You can meet a kitten before applying as long as their profile lists them as Available or Returned — Available. Visit our Events & Locations page to see where we will be next and learn about upcoming meet-and-greet opportunities.

What if there isn’t an adoption event scheduled?

You may still be able to meet our kittens through a private meet-and-greet, subject to availability. To request one:

  • Submit an adoption application for the kitten(s) you’re interested in through No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue.
  • After submitting your application, visit our Events & Locations page and complete the private meet-and-greet request form.
  • Use the form to tell us which kittens you’re interested in and your preferred date and time. We’ll follow up about scheduling.
Where do adoption events take place?

Adoption events are generally held on weekends, with occasional special events at other locations. Schedules can change, so please check our Events & Locations page for the current schedule and special-event details.

How do I apply to adopt?

There are two ways to get an adoption application:

  • Request one from the rescue: Visit No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue’s contact page and ask the rescue to send you the appropriate application and instructions.
  • Download an application: Visit the rescue’s adoption forms page to download an editable application or a printable copy.

Once you have your application:

  • Complete the editable application electronically, or print and fill out the paper copy by hand.
  • Email your completed application to nopawsleftbehindkittyrescue@gmail.com, or bring a completed paper application to an in-person adoption event.

For the full process, visit How to Adopt.

Who handles the adoption?

We are a foster team working in partnership with No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The kittens are placed in our home through the rescue.

The rescue handles:

  • Adoption applications, reviews, and approvals
  • Adoption contracts and fees
  • Finalizing adoptions

Our foster team provides:

  • A safe, loving foster home
  • Time to get to know each kitten and their personality
  • Help finding the right match for your home
What is the adoption fee?

Single kitten: $200
Pair of kittens: $350

Occasionally, special adoption promotions may be available. Adoption fees and promotions are determined by No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue and may vary.

For questions about adoption fees or available promotions, please contact No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue through its contact page.

What comes home with an adopted kitten?

When the adoption is finalized and your kitten is ready to go home, they will come with a blue take-home folder containing:

  • Spay or neuter paperwork
  • Vaccine card
  • Microchip number and registration information
  • Foster contact information
  • Kitten care guide
  • Adoption certificate
  • Pet Food Express coupon
  • A PetSmart coupon booklet may also be included for adoptions completed at PetSmart.

Carrier required: A cat carrier is required for every kitten going home. Please bring your own carrier or arrange to purchase one; neither the rescue nor our foster team provides carriers.

What if I’m interested in a kitten who isn’t ready yet?

Kittens labeled Coming Soon or Almost Available are not currently available for adoption, and adoption applications are not being accepted yet.

  • Coming Soon: These kittens are often still very young and are growing, developing their personalities, and learning in foster care.
  • Almost Available: These kittens are getting closer, but still need more time before they can be listed as Available.

Please check back for updates. An inquiry does not promise or reserve a kitten.

Can I apply for a kitten listed as Pending Interest?

What the status means:

  • An adoption application is currently being considered.
  • The applicant may still be completing part of the adoption process or another step with the rescue.
  • Other people may still submit adoption applications during this time.

How applications are considered:

  • The rescue reviews completed applications in the order they are received.
  • If the first applicant is not approved or does not move forward, the next completed applicant may be considered.
  • An inquiry or request for an application does not put you in line. Your application must be fully completed and submitted to the rescue to be considered.
  • The rescue will review your application and let you know its decision.

Please keep in mind:

  • Our foster kittens regularly attend weekend adoption events.
  • At an event, an interested person may submit an application, have it reviewed and approved, and complete the remaining adoption steps so the kitten can go home that same day.
  • An inquiry or an application that has not yet been approved does not reserve a kitten.
Can I put a kitten on hold?

One-week hold:

  • If your adoption application has been approved and you need extra time before bringing your kitten(s) home, a one-week hold may be possible with rescue approval.
  • A $50 deposit per kitten must be paid to No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue.
  • The deposit is non-refundable because the hold removes the kitten from consideration by other adopters and prevents them from attending adoption events during that time.
  • The deposit will be applied toward the final adoption fee.

Hold expiration and extensions:

  • A hold lasts seven days and expires at the end of that period.
  • Depending on when the hold begins, the next weekend adoption event may be approaching as the hold period ends.
  • Paws & Purr Fosters or the rescue may message you to check whether you are still interested and let you know the kitten may return to an upcoming event once the hold expires. A follow-up message does not automatically extend the hold.
  • If you do not respond or stay in communication, the hold will expire; we may not send additional reminders.
  • If you need more time, contact Paws & Purr Fosters before the next adoption weekend. Any extension must be discussed in advance with Paws & Purr Fosters and approved by the rescue.
  • A hold is not confirmed until the rescue approves it and its payment instructions have been followed.

When the adoption is finalized:

  • If your application and contract have been approved and the full adoption fee has been paid, but you need additional time before pickup, contact Paws & Purr Fosters or the rescue to discuss arrangements.
  • The rescue must approve any pickup arrangements.
What does Returned — Available mean?

Returned — Available means a kitten was previously adopted and has since returned to foster care.

  • Adjustment period: They may need extra patience and time to settle back into their familiar routine. We generally allow around a week to a month, although this can be shorter or longer depending on the kitten.
  • Returning to adoption events: While they are in our care, we observe how they are settling in and let the rescue know when we feel they are ready to return to adoption events.
  • Every kitten is different: A returned kitten may benefit from a patient home and a little extra understanding as they adjust to a new environment again.

If you have a question about whether a particular kitten is ready to attend an event again, please reach out.
