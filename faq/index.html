Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our foster kittens or the adoption process? Here are a few helpful answers. If your question isn’t answered here, or you have a question about one of our kittens, you’re welcome to send us a message below.

Do bonded pairs need to be adopted together?

Yes. Kittens labeled as a bonded pair must be adopted together, regardless of age. Some kittens may have a preferred pair listed on their profile, or may be able to go home with one of their siblings or foster siblings. In special cases, a singleton may be adopted on their own, while another singleton may need to go to a home with another cat. The appropriate pairing requirement will be clearly listed on each kitten’s profile. The majority of our kittens need to go home in pairs. No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue’s policy also requires kittens six months old and younger to be adopted in pairs.

Can I meet a kitten before applying?

Yes! You can meet a kitten before applying as long as their profile lists them as Available or Returned — Available. Visit our Events & Locations page to see where we will be next and learn about upcoming meet-and-greet opportunities.

Where do adoption events take place?

Adoption events are generally held on weekends, with occasional special events at other locations. Schedules can change, so please check our Events & Locations page for the current schedule and special-event details.

How do I apply to adopt?

There are two ways to get an application. You can visit No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue’s contact page and ask the rescue to send you the appropriate application and instructions. Or, you can visit the rescue’s adoption forms page to download either an editable application to complete electronically or a printable copy to fill out by hand and bring to an in-person event. Complete the editable application electronically or print and fill it out by hand. Email your completed application to nopawsleftbehindkittyrescue@gmail.com, or bring a completed paper application to an in-person adoption event.

For the full process, visit How to Adopt.

Who handles the adoption?

We are a foster team working in partnership with No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The kittens are placed in our home through the rescue. All adoption applications, application reviews and approvals, contracts, adoption fees, and final adoptions are handled by the rescue. Our role is to provide a safe, loving foster home, get to know each kitten, and help you find the right match for your home.

What comes home with an adopted kitten?

When the adoption is finalized and your kitten is ready to go home, they will come with a blue take-home folder containing their spay or neuter paperwork, vaccine card, microchip number and registration information, foster contact information, and a kitten care guide. The folder also includes an adoption certificate and a Pet Food Express coupon. Adoptions completed at PetSmart may include a PetSmart coupon booklet. A cat carrier is required for every kitten going home. Please bring your own carrier or arrange to purchase one; neither the rescue nor our foster team provides carriers.

What if I’m interested in a kitten who isn’t ready yet?

Kittens labeled Coming Soon or Almost Available are not currently available for adoption and are not accepting adoption applications. Coming Soon kittens are often still very young and are growing, developing their personalities, and learning in foster care. Almost Available kittens are getting closer, but still need more time before they can be listed as Available. Please check back for updates. No kitten is promised or reserved based on an inquiry.

Can I apply for a kitten listed as Pending Interest?

What the status means: An adoption application is currently being considered. The applicant may be completing part of the adoption process or another step with the rescue. Other people may still submit adoption applications during this time.

How applications are considered: The rescue reviews completed applications in the order they are received. If the first applicant is not approved or does not move forward, the next completed applicant may be considered, and so on. An inquiry or request for an application does not put you in line. To be considered, your application must be fully completed and submitted to the rescue. The rescue will review it and let you know its decision.

Please keep in mind: Our foster kittens regularly attend weekend adoption events. At an event, an interested person may complete and submit an application, have it reviewed and approved, and complete the remaining adoption steps so the kitten can go home that same day. An inquiry or an application that has not yet been approved does not reserve a kitten.

Can I put a kitten on hold?

One-week hold: If your adoption application has been approved and you need extra time before bringing your kitten(s) home, a one-week hold may be possible with approval from the rescue. A $50 deposit per kitten must be paid to No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The deposit is non-refundable because the hold removes the kitten from consideration by other adopters and prevents them from attending adoption events during that time. The deposit will be applied toward the final adoption fee.

Hold expiration and extensions: A hold lasts for seven days and expires at the end of that period. Depending on when the hold begins, the next weekend adoption event may be approaching as the hold period ends. Paws & Purr Fosters or the rescue may send you a message to check whether you are still interested and let you know that the kitten may return to an upcoming event once the hold expires. A follow-up message does not automatically extend the hold. If you do not respond or stay in communication, the hold will expire; we may not send additional reminders. If you need more time, please contact Paws & Purr Fosters before the next adoption weekend so we can discuss your situation. Any extension must be discussed in advance with Paws & Purr Fosters and approved by the rescue. A hold is not confirmed until the rescue approves it and its payment instructions have been followed.

When the adoption is finalized: If your adoption application and contract have been approved and the full adoption fee has been paid, but you need additional time before pickup, please contact Paws & Purr Fosters or the rescue to discuss arrangements. Ultimately, the rescue must approve any pickup arrangements.

What does Returned — Available mean?

Returned — Available means a kitten was previously adopted and has since returned to foster care. They may need extra patience and time to settle back into their familiar routine. We generally allow around a week to a month for this adjustment, although it can be shorter or longer depending on the kitten. As they are in our care, we observe how they are settling in and let the rescue know when we feel they are ready to return to adoption events. If you have a question about whether a particular kitten is ready to attend an event again, please reach out. Every kitten is different, and a returned kitten may benefit from a patient home and a little extra understanding as they adjust to a new environment once again.
