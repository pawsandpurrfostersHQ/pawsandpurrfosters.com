Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our foster kittens or the adoption process? Here are a few helpful answers. All adoptions are completed through No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue.

Do bonded pairs need to be adopted together?

Yes. Kittens labeled as a bonded pair must be adopted together, regardless of age. Some kittens may have a preferred pair listed on their profile, or may be able to go home with one of their siblings or foster siblings. In special cases, a singleton may be adopted on their own, while another singleton may need to go to a home with another cat. The appropriate pairing requirement will be clearly listed on each kitten’s profile. The majority of our kittens need to go home in pairs. No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue’s policy also requires kittens six months old and younger to be adopted in pairs.

Can I meet a kitten before applying?

Yes! You can meet a kitten before applying as long as their profile lists them as Available or Returned — Available. Visit our Events & Locations page to see where we will be next and learn about upcoming meet-and-greet opportunities.

Where do adoption events take place?

Adoption events are generally held on weekends, with occasional special events at other locations. Schedules can change, so please check our Events & Locations page for the current schedule and special-event details.

How do I apply to adopt?

There are two ways to get an application. You can visit No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue’s contact page and ask the rescue to send you the appropriate application and instructions. Or, you can visit the rescue’s adoption forms page to download either an editable application to complete electronically or a printable copy to fill out by hand and bring to an in-person event. Whether you complete the application electronically or by hand, follow the rescue’s instructions for submitting it. You may also bring a completed paper application to an in-person adoption event.

For the full process, visit How to Adopt.

Who handles the adoption?

We are a foster team working in partnership with No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The kittens are placed in our home through the rescue. All adoption applications, application reviews and approvals, contracts, adoption fees, and final adoptions are handled by the rescue. Our role is to provide a safe, loving foster home, get to know each kitten, and help you find the right match for your home.

What comes home with an adopted kitten?

When the adoption is finalized and your kitten is ready to go home, they will come with a blue take-home folder containing their spay or neuter paperwork, vaccine card, microchip number and registration information, foster contact information, and a kitten care guide. The folder also includes an adoption certificate and a Pet Food Express coupon. Adoptions completed at PetSmart may include a PetSmart coupon booklet. A cat carrier is required for every kitten going home. Please bring your own carrier or arrange to purchase one; neither the rescue nor our foster team provides carriers.

What if I’m interested in a kitten who isn’t ready yet?

Kittens labeled Coming Soon or Almost Available are not currently available for adoption and are not accepting adoption applications. Coming Soon kittens are often still very young and are growing, developing their personalities, and learning in foster care. Almost Available kittens are getting closer, but still need more time before they can be listed as Available. Please check back for updates. No kitten is promised or reserved based on an inquiry.

Can I apply for a kitten listed as Pending Interest?

Yes. Pending Interest means a completed adoption application is currently being considered. The applicant may be completing the adoption process or another step with the rescue, but other people may still submit applications during this time. The rescue reviews completed applications in the order they are received. If the first applicant is not approved or does not move forward, the next completed application may be considered, and so on. Simply asking for an application or making an inquiry does not put you in line; the application must be completed and submitted to the rescue. Please also keep in mind that our foster kittens often attend weekend adoption events, where an available kitten may be adopted that same day. A kitten may therefore be adopted at an event even if another person has made an inquiry or is waiting to hear back about an application.

Can I put a kitten on hold?

In some situations, a hold may be possible after your adoption application has been approved. If you need additional time before bringing your kitten(s) home, contact Paws & Purr Fosters to discuss the timing and arrangements. The rescue must approve the arrangement.

For an approved application, Paws & Purr Fosters may be able to hold a kitten for up to one week after a $50 deposit per kitten has been paid to No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue. The deposit is non-refundable because the hold takes the kitten out of consideration for other potential adopters and keeps them from attending adoption events during that period. The deposit will be applied toward the final adoption fee.

If you need more time, please communicate with Paws & Purr Fosters before the hold expires. Any extension or alternate arrangement must be discussed in advance and approved by the rescue. A hold is not confirmed until the rescue has approved it and its payment instructions have been followed.

What does Returned — Available mean?

Returned — Available means a kitten was previously adopted and has since returned to foster care. They may need extra patience and time to settle back into their familiar routine. We generally allow around a week to a month for this adjustment, although it can be shorter or longer depending on the kitten. As their foster parent, I observe how they are settling in and let the rescue know when I feel they are ready to return to adoption events. Every kitten is different, and a returned kitten may benefit from a patient home and a little extra understanding as they adjust to a new environment again.

Still have questions? Visit our How to Adopt page or contact No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue for adoption-process questions.
